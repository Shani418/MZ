# Physics and Quantum Foundations

This section examines physics relevant to the consciousness investigation without assuming that physical terminology automatically establishes metaphysical conclusions.

## Topics
- quantum superposition
- measurement
- decoherence
- interpretations of quantum mechanics
- emergence
- information in physics
- causality
- locality and nonlocality
- time
- statistical mechanics

## Boundary
A physical phenomenon and a philosophical interpretation of that phenomenon must be recorded separately.
