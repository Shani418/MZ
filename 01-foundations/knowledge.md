# Knowledge

## Status
Central unresolved investigation.

## Starting questions
- What is knowledge?
- How is knowledge possible?
- Why is there knowledge?
- When does something qualify as knowledge?
- Can knowledge know itself?
- Is knowledge inside consciousness, or does consciousness arise within a structure of knowledge?

## Recursive problem
The user described knowledge as potentially becoming a "trap inside a trap" for consciousness and wisdom.

The methodological warning is:

**Do not mistake an interpretation or justification for direct acquaintance with what is being investigated.**

## Epistemic discipline
"I do not know" is treated as potentially more than humility. It can be disciplined uncertainty: refusing to fill an unresolved gap with an unsupported conclusion.

## Questions to develop
- Is knowledge fundamentally propositional, procedural, embodied, relational, experiential, or something else?
- Does knowledge require a subject and object?
- Can there be knowledge without interpretation?
- Is certainty necessary for knowledge?
- Is knowledge a relation, a state, a process, or a structure?
- What is the relationship among knowledge, truth, justification, perception, and wisdom?
