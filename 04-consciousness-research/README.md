# Consciousness Research

This section will track contemporary scientific theories and empirical evidence concerning consciousness.

## Candidate frameworks to investigate
- Global Neuronal Workspace / Global Workspace approaches
- Integrated Information Theory
- Higher-Order theories
- Recurrent Processing approaches
- Predictive Processing and related frameworks
- Attention Schema approaches
- Active inference and related models
- Higher-level cognitive and metacognitive accounts

## Research rule
For current claims, record publication date, evidence type, population/system studied, major predictions, limitations, and competing interpretations.

Do not collapse competing theories into a single consensus when the evidence does not justify doing so.
