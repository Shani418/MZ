# The Repository Archive

This file is the entry point for the evolving long-term archive of our investigation into consciousness, knowledge, philosophy, science, and related domains.

The structure is deliberately provisional and will develop as the conversation develops. New ideas must preserve their context, chronology, distinctions, and uncertainty rather than being prematurely treated as settled conclusions.

## Current core thread
- consciousness as a possible fundamental starting point
- awareness and self-awareness
- perception/acquaintance versus interpretation
- knowledge as the central unresolved question
- knowledge about knowledge and recursive epistemic traps
- wisdom and its relation to knowledge
- the user's hypothesis that every particle is conscious
- quantum superposition as part of the user's reasoning, kept distinct from the empirical claim that particles are conscious

## Archive rule
Separate user hypotheses, established science, philosophical positions, competing theories, evidence, and open questions. The foundation remains intentionally revisable.
