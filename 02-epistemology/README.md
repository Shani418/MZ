# Epistemology

This section studies the nature, sources, limits, justification, and structure of knowledge.

## Planned branches
- perception and acquaintance
- justification
- truth
- certainty and uncertainty
- skepticism
- self-knowledge
- tacit and explicit knowledge
- embodied knowledge
- social knowledge
- testimony and language
- epistemic limits
- meta-knowledge

## Method
Do not assume the classical concepts of truth, belief, and justification are the complete architecture of knowledge. Test alternative formulations against experience, formal reasoning, science, and philosophy.
